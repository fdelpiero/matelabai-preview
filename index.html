      2. Leak plus “today” means urgent. The service guide says emergency call-outs run until 6 pm, with a $150 call-out fee.

      4. Rule check: moving a booking within the same week is allowed without approval.

      5. Drafts a friendly reply offering both times. Tom picks 8:00.

      6. Moves the visit to Friday at 8:00, updates the CRM and frees up the Thursday slot.

      1. WhatsApp · 18:30

        “How much for a full bathroom reno in Manly?”

        New enquiry

      2. Price guide: renovations are quoted after a site visit. Past jobs show no two renos cost the same.

      3. Rule: never give a renovation price without a person. The agent does not offer a number.

      5. Hands over to Jess in the office with a two-line summary, and saves the lead in the CRM.

        A few good questions

        Before we get started.

        Do we need to know how to code?

        No. We build and document the solution, then train your team to use it. We agree who maintains it and what support is useful before we start.

        Will we need to replace our existing tools?

        Usually not. We start with the tools you already use and check what can connect. If something new is needed, we discuss it while scoping.

        How much will it cost?

        It depends on the workflow and integrations. We agree the scope with you before a build begins and talk through the costs, including any ongoing tool costs.

        What happens to our business data?

        We agree what information is needed, who can access it and which tools process it. Access and human-review requirements are part of the scope.
