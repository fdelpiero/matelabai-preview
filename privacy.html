Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: we collect what we need to reply to you and to work with you, we look after it, and we don’t sell it.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Who we are

Mate Lab AI is an AI automation consultancy based in Sydney, Australia. The business is run by F Mora Castro & J.M Suarez, a partnership trading as Mate Lab AI (ABN 38 122 840 362). For any privacy question or request, email info@matelabai.com.au.

What we collect

We collect the information you give us when you fill in a form on this website, book a call, email us, come to one of our events or work with us. That is usually your name, contact details, company and what you would like help with.

Our enquiry form has two steps. If you complete the first step and leave before finishing, the first step is still sent to us so we can follow up.

Like most websites, ours also records basic technical information about visits, such as pages viewed and the type of device and browser.

Please don’t send us sensitive information, such as health details. We don’t need it to help you.

How we use it

We use personal information to reply to you, arrange calls, prepare proposals, deliver and invoice our services, keep our website working and secure, and meet our legal obligations.

We only send updates and tips if you asked for them, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We don’t sell personal information.

Information clients share with us

When a client gives us information for a project, we use it for that project and as the client instructs. Our work often involves AI and automation tools. If your information is confidential or needs special handling, tell us before we start and we will agree how to treat it.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our business, for things like website hosting, email, forms, call bookings, our customer records, automation and AI tools. They handle information on our behalf. Some of them store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, people or systems.

Cookies and analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand how our website is used. Some tools shown on the site come from other companies, such as the Google calendar for booking a call, and they may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

Security and how long we keep it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information and keep it only for as long as we reasonably need it for our work and our legal obligations. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or raise a privacy concern, by emailing info@matelabai.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
